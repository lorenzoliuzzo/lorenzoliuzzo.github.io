{% comment %} Summary strip for a travel entry: how the trip was made, when, and how far it got. Rendered both under a trip's own title (from page__meta.html) and inside its card on the archive, so the two never drift apart — the same contract book-meta.html has with the library. Every part is optional: a trip with nothing but a title and one stop still renders cleanly. The countries line is derived from the stops rather than being a second field to keep in sync.
