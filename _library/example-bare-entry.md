A title and an author are all a book needs. No shelf, no date, no rating, no
write-up: the card renders as "Read", with nothing invented to fill the gaps. That
matters more than it sounds — a book logged from memory years later genuinely has no
finish date, and printing today's would be a small lie the rest of this site is built
to avoid.
